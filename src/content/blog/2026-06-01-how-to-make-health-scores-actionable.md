---
title: How to make health scores actionable
description: Health scores only help when they point to evidence, owners, and next actions.
date: "2026-06-01T00:00:00Z"
category: Operating Systems
draft: false
---

A score only matters when it is tied to evidence, ownership, and action.

Teams do better when each health shift answers three questions:

1. What changed?
2. Who owns the next move?
3. What signal would change this status again?

If you can't answer those, the color is meaningless — and so is the meeting.
